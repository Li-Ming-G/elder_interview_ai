## Goal

Enforce one deterministic deadline/error surface and guarantee that background memory work never blocks or corrupts the live interview lane.

## Required Behavior

1. The accepted generation deadline applies to the accepted whole-generation boundary and is checked consistently at publication-sensitive seams.
2. Deadline expiry fails as `SYSTEM_ERROR`/unavailable according to the accepted compatibility surface; it must never become a fallback question or `CONTINUE_LISTENING`.
3. P3/P4/context/evidence/provider/tool/runtime errors remain distinguishable in safe diagnostics while sharing the accepted public fail-closed behavior.
4. Genuine Director `CONTINUE_LISTENING` remains a successful semantic result distinct from any error path.
5. P2 background pending, timeout or failure never blocks Director execution, recording, finalized transcript processing or manual-next.
6. Background completion after a generation freezes cannot mutate that generation's P4/context/evidence membership.
7. P1/P2 errors cannot cause partial question publication or weaken the P6R-03 fence.
8. Error paths finalize durable attempts/jobs/traces consistently and idempotently.
9. Restart/orphan recovery remains deterministic and fail-closed.
10. Raw transcript/evidence bodies are not copied into logs or Decision Trace beyond already accepted safe-reference behavior.
11. Existing recording/transcription completion behavior remains available even when question/memory background work fails.
